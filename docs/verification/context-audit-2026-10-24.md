# Context audit 2026-10-24

Random sample of 40 cited statements from 10928 in verified entries (seed 2026-10-24). Judged by four independent passes that did not write or verify the entries; items they could not open were re-read by the editor where an open copy existed.

Result: 34 ok, 3 narrower, 1 misread, 2 unreadable (38 judged; 4 of 38 needed a change, all corrected the same day).

## 1. drying – ok

> In a microscopy study, damage at 60 °C was already visible when the coffee still held 20% moisture, while sun-dried coffee and coffee dried at 40 °C were undamaged at 11%.

- s7: Microscopia eletrônica de varredura do endosperma de café (Coffea arabica L.) durante o processo de secagem – https://doaj.org/article/1d3b3b4c9783454f8b4b0f1991eef4b5

Note: Saath et al. 2010 (Cienc. Agrotec. 34(1)), C. arabica, natural and pulped coffee pre-dried on a patio and then dried on the patio or in fixed-layer dryers at 40 and 60 °C down to 11% (wb). The abstract states that the cytoplasm at 11% was not affected by patio/sun or 40 °C drying, and that at 60 °C changes were seen in coffee at 20% moisture. Read from the abstract (UNESP/Sumarios records); the DOAJ link itself did not load and the full text was not opened.
